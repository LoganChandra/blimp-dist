# Distribution probe — reality votes before money scales

## Design rules
1. **Pre-register or it didn't happen.** Thresholds (CPC, visit→activation,
   activation→paid) written into the decision log BEFORE the first dollar.
   Post-hoc rationalization is the indie killer — the verdict is computed, not felt.
2. **Probe the riskiest assumption first** (from the vetting verdict) — usually
   CPC reality or willingness-to-pay, both measurable for a few hundred dollars.
3. **Land on a REAL tool, not a fake door.** Thin product first (a weekend
   build); concierge behind the curtain if needed. Fake doors poison trust and
   measure the wrong thing.
4. **Exact match, manual CPC, no smart bidding** during the probe — you're
   buying DATA, not conversions. No conclusions before ~100 clicks/ad-group.
5. **Budget $300-600, 10-14 days.** Enough for signal on 2-3 ad groups; small
   enough to be tuition if it fails.

## Keyword thesis (L-008)
Build ad groups around self-labeling moments — queries where the artifact in the
user's hand names the search. Negatives from day 1: evasion-intent, free-only,
navigational, jobs/courses.

## The verdict (logged as a numbered decision)
| Outcome | Rule | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **GO** | ≥2 of 3 thresholds pass | Scale spend stepwise (never >35% of MRR), ship the retention engine, begin the weekly ritual |
| **ITERATE** | 1 of 3 passes | ONE funnel surgery (hook copy, price, page speed — one variable), re-probe with remaining budget |
| **REVIEW** | 0 of 3 after one iteration | Kill-criteria analysis; nearest-pivot scan (SEO library, engine, audience usually survive a pivot); PARK or pivot, packaged for the Boss |

## After GO — the two-engine doctrine
Ads buy data; SEO compounds it. Every converting search term becomes a content
page; organic share is a first-class KPI (target ≥40% by month 6). Ads spend is
forever capped at ≤ MRR × 0.35.
